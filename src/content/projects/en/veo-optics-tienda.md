problem: The store sold through social media, and every order meant a long conversation about models, prices and availability. Seasonal promotions were shared as loose images and there was no way to measure what worked.
solution: An online store with a catalog by category, brand and shape, a self-managed campaign banner, online payments and the option to book an in-store appointment to get measured before buying.
process:
  - title: Catalog and photography
    text: We organized the inventory by line, brand and material, and defined a consistent photo format for the whole store.
  - title: Store design
    text: A sober black-and-white look with the brand’s light blue as accent. The product page was designed mobile-first.
  - title: Payments, shipping and appointments
    text: We integrated card payments, home delivery, in-store pickup and a flow to book eye exams.
  - title: Campaigns
    text: We left banners, coupons and collections ready for Black Friday and key dates, all switchable without touching code.
decisions:
  - title: Shopify instead of custom development
    text: The team needed autonomy and low maintenance. Shopify handles payments, inventory and security, so we could focus on the experience.
  - title: The appointment as part of the purchase
    text: With prescription glasses, measurements matter. Offering the appointment within the purchase flow reduced the hesitation to buy online.
  - title: Measure from day one
    text: Analytics and conversion events were installed before launch to plan campaigns with data.
highlights:
  - Online sales with no prior conversation
  - Promotions the team switches on by itself
  - Eye exams booked from the store
  - Data on which products and campaigns convert
learnings:
  - A good product page answers the questions that used to arrive by chat
  - Client autonomy is a feature, not an extra
