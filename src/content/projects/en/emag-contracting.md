problem: The company found clients through referrals and had no online presence to show its work or make it easy to request a quote.
solution: A fast, direct presentation site that showcases real projects, builds trust and leads visitors to request a quote in just a few steps.
process:
  - title: Brief and references
    text: We defined who the site speaks to, what it should achieve and which visual style represents the company.
  - title: Design
    text: A structure centered on finished work, with large photos and short messages.
  - title: Development
    text: Built with Astro and Tailwind, reusable components and GSAP entrance animations.
  - title: Optimization and delivery
    text: Optimized images, technical SEO and mobile testing before publishing.
decisions:
  - title: A static site with Astro
    text: No database or server to maintain. It loads fast, is cheap to host and has almost no attack surface.
  - title: Measured animation
    text: GSAP gives each section rhythm on entrance, without effects that distract from the work being shown.
highlights:
  - A static, fast and easy-to-maintain site
  - A call to action visible throughout the journey
  - Design adapted to mobile
learnings:
  - On a commercial site, every section should bring the visitor closer to contact
  - Good photography does more for a design than any effect
